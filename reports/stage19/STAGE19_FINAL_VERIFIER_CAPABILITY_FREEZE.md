# Stage 19 Final Verifier Capability Freeze

Stage 19 is frozen as a deterministic research-only verifier. Exact canonical textual
verification and provenance validation are accepted. Anatomical verification is limited to
governed proxy evidence and may produce at most `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED`. Reliable positive
lesion localization, laterality inference, contradiction from localization absence, heuristic
identity matching, and all frozen unsupported clinical domains remain prohibited.
