# Stage 15C Severity Capability Freeze

Stage 15 severity modeling is scientifically withheld, not failed. Existing governed evidence contains no explicit ordinal severity labels and no prospectively approved, validated finding-specific quantitative definition.

No severity labels were created. Stage 10 localization area, Stage 12 technical-quality proxies, and classifier probabilities remain prohibited severity sources. Reopening requires governed radiologist ordinal severity labels, quantitative severity measurements, or an approved and validated finding-specific definition.
