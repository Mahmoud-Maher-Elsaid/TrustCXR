# Stage 11L Fusion Acceptance Decision

Stage 11L is a no-inference decision gate over the complete Stage 11K shared-validation evidence. It may accept the fusion layer only as a research uncertainty annotation mechanism.

The observed 106 `UNCERTAIN` and 2 `UNLOCALIZED` records demonstrate no reliable positive fusion support. The Stage 10 localization operating point remains unaccepted, so the localizer cannot contradict the classifier. The maximum permitted support status remains `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`, although no such status was observed.

This decision does not authorize clinical localization, test evaluation, retraining, tuning, or modification of frozen Stage 9/10 results.
