# Stage 11I Fusion Coverage Decision

Stage 11I applies a no-inference coverage gate to the Stage 11H aggregate evidence. Complete coverage of the 108-record shared validation cohort is required before a representative fusion conclusion because the 19-record overlap was not designed as an independent representative sample.

At least one classifier-positive record is required to assess the partial-support pathway. Stage 11H contains none. The Stage 10 localization operating point also remains unaccepted, so localization cannot be treated as reliable contradiction evidence.

This stage does not generate new Stage 9 predictions, retrain either model, alter frozen evaluations, or access locked-test data. Its expected conservative outcome is a hold for later shared-validation prediction-coverage preparation.
