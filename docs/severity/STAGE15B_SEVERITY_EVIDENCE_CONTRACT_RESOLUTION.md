# Stage 15B Severity Evidence and Contract Resolution

Stage 15B audits governed metadata profiles and frozen reports without reading pixels or locked-test records. It distinguishes explicit ground-truth severity, prospectively validated quantitative severity proxies, and unsupported heuristic severity.

No mild, moderate, or severe labels are created. Stage 10 localization area, Stage 12 technical-quality proxies, classifier probabilities, and narrative adjectives without an approved annotation contract are not severity ground truth. If neither explicit annotations nor a validated finding-specific definition exists, Stage 15 is scientifically withheld rather than failed.
