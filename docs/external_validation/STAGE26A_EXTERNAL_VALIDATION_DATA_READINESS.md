# Stage 26A External Validation Data Readiness

Stage 26A applies a strict capability-specific external-validation definition. Internal held-out
splits and datasets used for training, validation, selection, calibration, fusion development,
or post-test decisions are not external cohorts.

The ten governed local datasets yield no eligible independent cohort. NIH, CheXmask, RSNA, and
CheXpert were used in relevant development; CheXpert also lacks the complete frozen Stage 9
label contract and cross-dataset patient non-overlap is not proven. VinBigData, Indiana, SIIM,
TBX11K, COVID Radiography, and CRD retain identity, join, license, or label-semantic blockers.

Accordingly, full Stage 9, Stage 13, Stage 10 localization, Stage 5, Stage 16 reliability, and
downstream external validation remain scientifically withheld. Stage 26B is the minimum formal
`WITHHELD_NOT_FAILED` closure. No acquisition, relabeling, inference, retraining, optional
capability expansion, or language-model work is required for project completion.
